## Policy reminder

Per [IGNORE_TEST_POLICY.md](IGNORE_TEST_POLICY.md), every `@Ignore` must carry:

1. A linked tracking issue (`@Ignore("blocked-on: #1234")`).
2. A category (`flaky`, `blocked-on`, `wip`).
3. A deadline label on the linked issue.

**None** of the audit-named six tests meet all three criteria today. The next sub-PR (#28-3, the `ignoreAudit` Gradle task) will fail CI for any `@Ignore` that doesn't comply. Filing tracking issues for the six is the *next* sub-PR after that (#28-4).
